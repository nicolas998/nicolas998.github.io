**Hillslope Playground: an interactive tool to analyze hillslope model formulations**

We are happy to announce the release of **Hillslope Playground**, a new web page to analyze hillslope model formulations described with ordinary differential equations (ODEs). The hillslope is the basic unit where runoff is generated before it reaches the river network, and the way we represent its storages and fluxes strongly controls the simulated hydrographs. With this tool, you can interactively explore how different hillslope formulations behave and how their parameters shape the runoff response, directly from your browser and without installing anything.

We expect Hillslope Playground to be useful for research, teaching, and for anyone curious about how hillslope-scale processes are represented in hydrological models such as the Hillslope Link Model (HLM).

You can access the app [here](https://hillslopeplayground.streamlit.app/).
